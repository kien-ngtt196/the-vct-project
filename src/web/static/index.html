Standings show up to 500 players per season. 2021 scores exclude preliminary qualifiers and include Last Chance Qualifiers. Roles use at least five classified maps. Flex means the leading role is below 60% and a second role accounts for at least 25%. HS% averages reported map values. K/D uses total kills and deaths.
