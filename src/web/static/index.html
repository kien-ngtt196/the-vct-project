HOW THE RANKING WORKS

Power, with context

A descriptive 2026 ranking, not a match probability or tournament prediction.

01
Season results 35%Completed match wins, weighted by event importance
02
Recent form 25%Recency-weighted last five matches
03
In-game execution 25%Weighted map wins (40%), round strength (40%), K/D (20%)
04
Opponent strength 15%Event-weighted average 2026 opponent win rate

Event multipliers: Champions 3×, Masters 2×, regional 1×. Recent form combines event importance with match recency. Tier is inferred from the tournament title; unrecognized event types use regional weight.

Team regions are inferred from the most frequent regional 2026 tier-one tournament listing in VLR. Regional strength is not part of the score; unknown metrics remain neutral rather than guessed.
